How to Book Your Ticket

Get your Bengaluru Metro QR ticket instantly via WhatsApp.

1

Save the Number

Add 8105556677 to your phone's contacts.

chat Open WhatsApp
2

Initiate Chat

Open WhatsApp, search for the contact, and send 'Hi'.

3

Select Options

From the menu, tap 'QR Tickets' and then 'Buy Ticket'.

4

Enter Details

Select your source and destination stations, along with the number of passengers.

5

Make Payment

Pay the fare securely using UPI or other supported credit/debit options.

6

Scan & Ride

You will receive a QR code digital ticket. Scan it at the automatic fare collection (AFC) gates at entry and exit.

percent

Discount

You get a 5% discount compared to buying traditional tokens at the station.

schedule

Validity

Mobile QR tickets are valid until the end of the metro's revenue service on the day of purchase.
