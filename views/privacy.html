Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. Offline First & Privacy Commitment

Metro Sathi Bengaluru is built as a fully client-side, offline-capable application. We believe your transit journeys and location data should remain entirely private. The application runs completely inside your browser and does not communicate with any external backend servers.

2. Geolocation & GPS Data

When you use the Live GPS Proximity Tracker, the application requests access to your device's location. This coordinate data is processed locally inside your browser to calculate distances to upcoming stations and send proximity alerts.

This location data is never collected, transmitted, stored, or shared with third parties or the application creators.

3. Local Session Storage

We use browser-standard sessionStorage to retain your active AI Chat Guide assistant questions and routing results. This allows you to browse other tabs and return without losing your active itinerary. This storage is completely local to your browser and can be cleared at any time by closing the tab or clearing your browser data.

4. Third-Party Links

Our application may contain links to official Namma Metro transit resources or external booking portals. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or contents of those external websites.
